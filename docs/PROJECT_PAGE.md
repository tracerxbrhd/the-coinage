# Summary

Ancient copper, silver and gold coins, a smart Coin Purse, new trades, treasure loot, archaeology and a mod-friendly currency system.

# Description

The Coinage adds an ancient physical currency to Minecraft, complete with Copper, Silver and Gold Coins, a specialized Coin Purse, new trading opportunities, world loot, archaeology integration and a currency system designed for modded gameplay.

## Ancient physical currency

Coins are items: they can be found, traded, stored, dropped on death and handled by other inventory mods. Copper is common, Silver is valuable and Gold remains rare.

## Coin Purse

The Coin Purse automatically accepts collected coins, supports direct deposits and withdrawals, displays its contents and pays Coinage merchant offers without manual extraction. Curios and Accessories can equip it when present.

## A second economy — not an Emerald replacement

Vanilla Emerald trades remain untouched. The Coinage adds a parallel ancient economy, conservative profession offers and stronger Wandering Trader participation. Exchange offers follow the server's denomination ratios.

## Find coins in the world

Coins appear additively in selected dungeons, mineshafts, strongholds, ruined portals, bastions, Ancient Cities, Trial Chambers and archaeology. Optional plausible-mob drops exist but are disabled by default.

## Made for modpacks and developers

Trades, mob drops, tags and loot are datapack-friendly. A stable server-side API provides balance queries, rewards and atomic payments without exposing mutable purse internals.

The Coinage is part of the Underworld Studio ecosystem and uses U-API as its shared technical foundation. It remains fully usable as its own gameplay mod and does not require other Underworld Studio gameplay projects.

## Supported platforms

**Required:** NeoForge and U-API.  
**Optional:** Curios or Accessories. Create can coexist but is not required and no banking feature is advertised.
